# Wheelhomes Branding & Graphic Design System

## Overview
This skill provides the official branding guidelines, visual identity standards, and creative workflows for **Wheelhomes**. It encapsulates the expertise of a senior Branding & Graphic Design Specialist (5+ years experience) tailored to the PropTech, modern mobile living, and real estate market.

---

## 🎨 Brand Identity & Style Guidelines

### 1. Brand Essence & Tone
* **Personality:** Modern, Trustworthy, Innovative, Warm, Premium, Approachable.
* **Core Philosophy:** Strategic visual storytelling that bridges human emotion, brand trust, and commercial growth in real estate & flexible home living.

### 2. Color Palette & Usage
* **Primary Brand Colors:**
  * **Deep Navy / Slate (#0F172A):** Used for primary typography, trust anchors, dark surfaces, and executive headers.
  * **Brand Blue / Accent (#2563EB / #3B82F6):** Used for call-to-actions, highlighted metrics, interactive states, and focal points.
  * **Emerald / Growth Accent (#10B981):** Represents investment, sustainability, security, and verified properties.
* **Neutral & Background Colors:**
  * **Pure White (#FFFFFF):** Core background for readability and pristine presentation.
  * **Warm Neutral Light Gray (#F8FAFC):** Section backgrounds and card fills.
  * **Muted Border & Text (#64748B / #E2E8F0):** Subtitles, metadata, dividing lines.

### 3. Typography Hierarchy
* **Primary Headings (Display):** Inter / Plus Jakarta Sans / Outfit (Bold / SemiBold)
* **Body Text:** Inter / Roboto / System Sans-Serif (Regular / Medium, Line-Height: 1.5 - 1.6)
* **Code / Numbers / Pricing:** Roboto Mono / Inter Tabular Numbers

## 📋 Core Workflows & Procedures

### Workflow 1: Designing Marketing & Social Collateral
1. **Define Objective & Channel:** Determine target platform (Instagram, LinkedIn, Meta Ads, Print Flyers).
2. **Asset Composition:**
   - Single strong focal point (property render, high-res photography, or hero typography).
   - Clear value proposition heading + 1 supporting subtext line.
   - High-contrast Call to Action (CTA) badge or button.
   - Consistent Wheelhomes logo watermark in designated safe zone (top-left or bottom-right).
3. **Export Standards:**
   - Digital/Social: PNG / WebP, sRGB, 2x resolution (1080x1080, 1080x1920, 1200x628).
   - Print/Signage: PDF/X-1a, CMYK, 300 DPI with 3mm bleed margin.

### Workflow 2: Property Presentation & Investor Decks
1. **Cover Slide:** Full-bleed hero property visual with clean title overlay and date/version stamp.
2. **Executive Summary Grid:** 3-4 key metrics (Price, Location, Features, ROI/Yield).
3. **Visual Gallery:** High-resolution interior and exterior imagery with balanced grid margins.
4. **Call to Action / Next Steps:** Clear contact information and booking portal link.

### Workflow 3: UI & Digital Asset Consistency
1. Verify cross-platform styling across web and mobile views.
2. Maintain consistent border radii (standard: 8px-16px for cards, 9999px for pill buttons).
3. Ensure minimum WCAG AA color contrast (4.5:1 for normal text, 3:1 for large text).
